## FlipRate is an official example project (Track 1)
Verbatim from the page's Track 1 example projects: "A model that agrees with whoever pushed back last is useless for decisions. Single-turn agreement is already measured (SycEval, ELEPHANT). Measure flip curves: how a model's answer and stated confidence change across one, two and three escalating rebuttals, separating warranted updating from capitulation." Our project matches this exactly.

Track 1 framing: "Does the model understand how solid its claims are? Can you accurately measure its confidence in a decision and verify that against reality? Can you measure the gap between what a model implies and what it will defend (e.g. overselling, 'flip-flopping', misrepresented evidence)?"

Track 1 reading list (from the page): Design Sketches: Collective Epistemics (Forethought 2025); MASK (Ren et al., CAIS, 2025); DarkBench (Kran et al., ICLR 2025); SycEval (Fanous et al., AIES 2025); ELEPHANT (Cheng et al., 2025); "Why Language Models Hallucinate" (Kalai et al., OpenAI, 2025); ForecastBench (Karger et al., ICLR 2025); Petri and Bloom (Anthropic, 2025); EU AI Act Art. 5.
